## The Art of Divine Dueling

Welcome to **God Duel**, where divine luck meets tactical precision. Draw from a deck of 30+ unique heavenly relics, guard against fatal strikes, and outwit the ancient AI deity.

### Pro Tactics:
1. **Never Waste Your Best Shield:** If the opponent attacks with a small 2-damage dagger, taking the 2 damage is often wiser than burning your 8-guard Aegis Shield.
2. **Watch the AI's MP:** Powerful miracles require MP. Track how much energy your opponent has before committing your defense.
3. **The Miracle Gamble:** When at low health, a desperate Prayer of Life can turn a guaranteed defeat into a legendary comeback.
